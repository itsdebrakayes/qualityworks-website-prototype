6. **HubSpot chat widget and tracking**, GA4, Meta pixel: consent and analytics plan required (Q-10).
7. **Clutch reviews** link and award badges; testimonial carousel.
8. **Blog category filtering** (`/blog/?_blog_categories=agile` is linked from service pages).

10. **Case-study “Read full case study” links** from service pages.
11. **All 99 blog permalinks, 17 case-study URLs and 42 page URLs** (see 06 URL mapping).
